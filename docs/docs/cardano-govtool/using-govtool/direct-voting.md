# Direct Voting

Direct Voting is where any ADA holder can vote on any Governance Action. To be eligible to vote, you must register:

1. You will need ADA in a compatible wallet ([see list of compatible wallets](./getting-started/compatible-wallets.md))
2. Your ADA balance in your wallet must be large enough to cover the refundable DRep deposit (set by the `drepDeposit` protocol parameter, currently 500 ADA; GovTool shows the exact amount before you register) plus transaction fees. If your stake key is not yet registered, the same transaction also registers it, which requires an additional refundable stake key deposit.
3. Click on "Register" in the "Become a Direct Voter" panel on the Dashboard to get started\
   ![](/img/gitbook/become%20a%20direct%20voter.webp)&#x20;
4. Review the "What this Means" screen, which shows the refundable deposit, then click "Register" and sign the transaction in your wallet.
5. Wait a few minutes.
6. When the transaction is completed on the chain, the dashboard will change to show that you are registered to vote.
7. You can now vote on any [Governance Action](./governance-actions/README.md)

Your deposit is refunded if you retire as a Direct Voter. If you later decide to become a DRep, you will not need to pay another deposit.

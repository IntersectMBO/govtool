# Change your Vote

1. **To change your vote:**
   1. Go to Governance Actions page,&#x20;
   2. Click on "Voted on my me"
   3.  Click on the "View Details"  button on the Action that you want to change.\


       <figure><img src="/img/gitbook/Artboard%20Copy%205.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
2.  **Change your vote** \


    <figure><img src="/img/gitbook/Artboard%20Copy%206.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
3.  **Change Vote**\
    Click on 'Change vote'\


    <figure><img src="/img/gitbook/Artboard%20Copy%207.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
4.  **STEP 4 - Wallet Confirmation**\
    You will be asked to sign and confirm the transaction in your wallet.\


    <figure><img src="/img/gitbook/Artboard%20Copy%208.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
5.  **Vote changed**\
    Once the transaction it's completed, the page will refresh automatically. You have now changed your vote on this Governance Action.\


    <figure><img src="/img/gitbook/Artboard%20Copy%20900.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

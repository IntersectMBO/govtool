# Support

Need help with GovTool? Here's where to look:

* **Guides**: step-by-step instructions for [Using GovTool](./using-govtool/README.md), including [connecting your wallet](./using-govtool/getting-started/connect-your-wallet-to-govtool.md), [delegating](./using-govtool/delegating/README.md) and [voting](./using-govtool/governance-actions/vote-on-governance-actions/README.md).
* **FAQs**: answers to common questions in the [FAQs](./faqs/README.md).
* **System status and known issues**: check the [System status](../overview/system-status.md) page.
* **Feedback chat**: click the "Feedback" button in the footer of GovTool to chat with the support team.
* **Report a bug**: see [How to submit a bug](../bugs-or-feature-suggestions/how-to-submit-a-bug.md).
* **Suggest a feature**: see [How to suggest a feature or submit an idea](../bugs-or-feature-suggestions/how-to-suggest-a-feature-or-submit-an-idea.md).

:::warning
The GovTool team will never ask for your seed phrase or private keys. Never share them with anyone.
:::

# Abstain from Every Vote

**Vote Abstain by default**. This option will show that you have participated in Governance, but you are not supporting a yes or no vote on proposals. Your Voting Power is excluded from the active voting stake used to calculate whether proposals pass.

## How to delegate to Abstain

1. Open the DRep Directory ("View DRep Directory" on the dashboard).
2. Under "Automated Voting Options", find "Abstain from Every Vote" and click "Delegate".
3. Sign the transaction in your wallet.

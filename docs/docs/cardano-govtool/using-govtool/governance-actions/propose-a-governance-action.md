# Propose a Governance Action

To be able to submit a proposal for Governance Action, you will need to connect your wallet to GovTool in order to verify your identity.&#x20;

Proposals are created in the **Proposals** section of GovTool (Governance Actions → Proposals), where they can be discussed and refined before being submitted on-chain.

The following Governance Action types can be created in Proposals: Info Action, Treasury requests, Updates to the Constitution, Motion of No Confidence, Committee updates, and Hard Fork. Protocol Parameter Change proposals can't be created in Proposals.

Please follow the step by step guideline

1. Navigate to [https://gov.tools/](https://gov.tools/)
2. Connect your wallet.

<figure><img src="/img/gitbook/image%20%286%29.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. In the menu, open "Governance Actions" → "Proposals" (or click "Propose" on the dashboard's "Propose a Governance Action" card).

<figure><img src="/img/gitbook/image%20%281%29%20%281%29.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Click "Verify your identity". You will be asked to sign a message with your wallet (this is not a transaction and costs no fee). See [Verification for Budget Proposals, Proposals, and discussions](../verification-for-budget-proposals-proposals-and-discussions.md).

<figure><img src="/img/gitbook/image%20%282%29%20%281%29.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Select your wallet and connect.
6. Click "Propose a Governance Action"

<figure><img src="/img/gitbook/proposal%201.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

7. Click "Create new Proposal"

<figure><img src="/img/gitbook/image%20%284%29%20%281%29.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

8. Please ensure that you fill in all the required fields. It is important to complete every mandatory section to proceed successfully. Field limits: Title 80 characters; Abstract 2,500 characters; Motivation and Rationale 12,000 characters each. References are optional.

<figure><img src="/img/gitbook/image%20%285%29%20%281%29.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

9. Once you have filled in all the required fields, please click "Continue" to proceed to the next step.

<figure><img src="/img/gitbook/image%20%286%29%20%281%29.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

10. Then click on "Submit". You can also click "Save Draft" to continue later.

<figure><img src="/img/gitbook/image%20%288%29.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

11. Optionally, before submitting the proposal as a Governance Action, you can add a poll as a temperature check. To do this, click on "Add Poll".

<figure><img src="/img/gitbook/image%20%287%29.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

12. When you are ready, click "Submit as Governance Action". You will be asked to store the metadata file yourself ([Storing Information offline](../storing-information-offline.md)), and to sign the transaction that pays the refundable Governance Action deposit.

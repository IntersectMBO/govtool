# Delegate to a DRep



1.  **Dashboard**\
    From the dashboard, click the “View DRep Directory” button in the “Delegate your Voting Power” panel.


    <figure><img src="/img/gitbook/Artboardmm.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
2.  **Delegation Flow**\
    In the DRep Directory you can search for a DRep by name or ID. You have two options for delegation: You can delegate directly by clicking "Delegate" (2) below, or you can see more details of the DRep by clicking "View Details" (1). If you choose "View Details", you will be able to delegate as well from the subsequent page.


    <div align="left"><figure><img src="/img/gitbook/Artboard.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>


3.  **Wallet Confirmation**\
    You will need to sign the transaction in your wallet. If your stake key is not registered yet, the same transaction also registers it, which requires a refundable stake key deposit.


    <div align="left"><figure><img src="/img/gitbook/Artboard%20Copy%2019.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>


4.  **Transaction in progress**\
    There will be a short wait while the transaction gets onto the blockchain


    <div align="left"><figure><img src="/img/gitbook/Artboard%20Copy%2020.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
5.  **Delegation Complete**\
    Once the transaction it's completed, the dashboard will show that you have delegated your Voting Power to a DRep.

    In the screen below, you can see that you have delegated your Voting Power and also the name and DRep ID that you've delegated to.&#x20;

    You can change your delegation to another DRep, or to one of the automated voting options (Abstain, or No Confidence) by clicking "Delegate to Another DRep".


    <div align="left"><figure><img src="/img/gitbook/Artboard%20Copy%2021.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

# Connect your wallet to GovTool



Open your Chrome browser, and navigate to [https://preview.gov.tools/](https://preview.gov.tools/)&#x20;

<figure><img src="/img/gitbook/voltaire%20logged%20out.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

To start using GovTool's functions, you need to connect your wallet.

Simply click the "Connect Wallet" button at the top of the page (or "Participate now" on the home page). You will see a "Connect your Wallet" pop-up showing the wallets available in your browser; select your chosen wallet to connect.&#x20;

<figure><img src="/img/gitbook/connect%20demos%20wallet%20to%20Voltaire.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You will be asked for permission to connect with GovTool. To proceed, agree to this.&#x20;

Allow access in your wallet (no transaction is required). If your wallet has more than one stake key, you will be asked to pick one ("Pick Stake Key"). You will then be taken to the dashboard.&#x20;

If your wallet is set to a different network than the GovTool site you opened, GovTool will ask you to switch networks. If your wallet does not support CIP-95, GovTool will not be able to connect to it (see [Compatible Wallets](./compatible-wallets.md)).
